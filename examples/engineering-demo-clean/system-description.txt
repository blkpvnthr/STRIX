SYNTH-CTRL System Description (document SYNTH-CTRL-DESC, revision A)

The SYNTH-CTRL demonstration plant regulates the temperature of a closed
coolant loop. Sensing, actuation, and supervision are split across six
line-replaceable units connected by a small deterministic data bus.

[part: PT-0001] The primary controller coordinates all closed-loop control.
It polls every attached sensor, schedules pump and valve commands, and
raises alarms to the operator panel when any reading leaves its band.

[part: PT-0002] The coolant pump circulates fluid through the loop at a
regulated flow rate. Its drive electronics accept speed demands from the
primary controller over the electrical power-and-command harness.

[part: PT-0006] The operator panel presents live pressure and temperature
readings and exposes the manual shutdown control required for maintenance.
